# Your lines for the Lifeline film

You record 7 short clips. The narrator (the generated voice) keeps the walkthrough between them: the product screens, the mainnet run, the attack lab, the numbers. When your clips are in, the film is re-cut around them.

## How to record (10 minutes of setup, once)

- Phone, landscape, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps. On a stand or stack of books, lens at eye level, about an arm and a half away.
- Face a window (light in front of you, not behind). Plain wall or a tidy room behind you.
- Quiet room. Use wired earphones with a mic, or keep the phone close; fans and AC off.
- Plain dark T-shirt. Look into the lens, not the screen.
- For each line: start recording, wait 1 second, say the line, wait 1 second, stop. Do 2 or 3 takes. Talk a little slower than feels natural.
- It's fine to change words so it sounds like you. Keep the numbers exactly as written.
- Name the files `F1_1.mp4`, `F1_2.mp4`, `F2_1.mp4`… and put them in `~/Videos/Lifeline/founder/`.
