How a rescue happens

From the money you hold to the margin that saves your position, in four steps. The last two run on their own, every 5 seconds.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Fund a reserve from any chain

    Pay with what you hold. 1Click swaps it into USDC on NEAR, in your own account, and one click moves it into the Lifeline vault.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Paint your lines on the hull

    When to warn you, when to rescue and what health to refill to, plus caps per rescue and per day and a cooldown. The vault stores them.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    A reading every 5 seconds

    The keeper reads health from Hyperliquid: cross account value divided by maintenance margin. Liquidation comes at 1.0.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    A line thrown, within your rules

    Below your trigger it gets a signed 1Click quote to your perps balance and asks the vault to pay it. The vault checks every rule again, on chain.

Safety notice · vault contract on NEAR

What a hacked keeper can't do

The rules live in the vault contract, where a hacked keeper still can't break them. Each code below is what the contract answers when it refuses.

It only pays your Hyperliquid address

The vault reads where the money goes from 1Click's signed quote: the deposit address, your Hyperliquid address, the amount and the refund account. Anything else is refused.

BAD_QUOTERECIPIENT_MISMATCH

It can't pass your caps

Per rescue, per 24 hours and a cooldown between rescues, enforced by the contract, not by the keeper's good behaviour.

OVER_PER_RESCUEOVER_DAILYCOOLDOWN

Its key does one thing

The keeper holds a function-call key that can call the vault's rescue method and nothing else. It can't withdraw, transfer or change a policy.

NOT_KEEPER

You stay in charge

Pause at once, with no deposit to attach. Unpause and withdraw any time, with a confirmation in your wallet.
